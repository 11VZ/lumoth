Lumoth Uploader

The private tool that publishes videos to the Lumoth channel and reads that channel's statistics.

Watch Lumoth

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the Lumoth channel, with the title, description and privacy setting chosen by the channel owner.
  • Confirms each upload went to the right channel with the right settings.
  • Reads that channel's video list and statistics, so its owner can see how the videos are doing.

Who can use it

Lumoth Uploader is run by the owner of the Lumoth channel, for that channel only. It is a program on the owner's own computer. There are no public accounts or sign-ups, and it refuses to work with any other channel.

How access works

The channel owner signs in with Google and approves access. The owner can remove that access at any time from their Google account permissions.

The source of the upload program is public so it can be reviewed.
